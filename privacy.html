Privacy Policy – Store Automation n8n Last updated: November 1, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how Metall Turbo (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects information through our app Store Automation n8n (the “App”). The App connects to Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram) to automate content publishing for our own business accounts. 1. Who we are Metall Turbo is an industrial spare parts store based in Fergana, Uzbekistan. Contact email: metalturbofergana@gmail.com 2. What the App does The App is an internal business tool. It publishes product posts (photos and captions) to Metall Turbo’s own Instagram and Facebook business accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it does not let other people sign up or log in. 3. Information we collect Through the Meta APIs, the App may access: Account information for our own Facebook Page and Instagram Business account, such as the account ID, name, and username Content we publish, such as post images, captions, and publish status Basic engagement data on our own posts, such as likes, comments, and reach, if this permission is granted Comments or messages sent to our business account, used only to reply to customers, if this permission is granted We do not collect passwords, payment information, or sensitive personal data. 4. How we use information We use this information only to: Publish product posts to our own business accounts Check whether a post was published successfully Answer customer questions about our products 5. Sharing of information We do not sell, rent, or trade any information. Data is processed only by the services needed to run the automation: Meta (Facebook/Instagram), our workflow tool n8n, and Google services where product data is stored. We share information only when the law requires it. 6. Data storage and security Access tokens and data are stored securely and are used only by Metall Turbo staff to run the automation. We keep data only as long as it is needed for the purposes above. 7. Data deletion You can ask us to delete any data the App holds about you at any time: Send an email to metalturbofergana@gmail.com Use the subject line “Data Deletion Request” Include your name and your Facebook or Instagram username We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also remove the App’s access at any time in Facebook under Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites. 8. Children’s privacy The App is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. 9. Changes to this policy We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will appear on this page with a new “Last updated” date. 10. Contact us If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at metalturbofergana@gmail.com.
